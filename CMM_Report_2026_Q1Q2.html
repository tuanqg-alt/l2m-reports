Community analysis and game operations · January 2026

Executive Summary

January 2026 saw a shift from initial excitement over expansion content (Aden Expansion, Tower of Insolence 3–4F) toward a deeper focus on complex progression mechanics and investment value assessment.

📦
Content Reception

Spirit Inscription, Blessed Bracelets, and Expertise drove intense discussion but created spending pressure and barriers for F2P players.

🗺️
Regional Sentiment Divergence

VN and TH maintained high engagement; ID, PH, and SGMY faced churn risks due to technical issues, bots, and disappointing drop rates.

ℹ️
Information Gap

Much negativity stemmed from lack of official guidance (NPC locations, timelines, system explanations), generating high demand for a knowledge hub.

💰
Economic Dynamics

TOI tickets fluctuated sharply (300 → 40 Diamonds in 24 hours), reflecting rapid market self-correction based on Combat Power (CP) thresholds.
