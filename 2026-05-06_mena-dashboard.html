Why Strategy = MMORPG Proxy

Strategy 4X games (Last War, Whiteout Survival, etc.) share core DNA with MMORPGs: guild/clan systems, territory wars, long-term player progression, P2W monetization, and deep social engagement. Their dominance at 24–36% of ALL MENA gaming revenue proves that the spending behavior and gameplay engagement patterns required for MMORPG success already exist in MENA — players just haven't been served a true MMORPG yet.

⚔️ VERDICT: MENA is VIABLE but UNPREPARED for Lineage
The spending capacity exists (Strategy 4X dominance across all markets, Gulf ARPU $1.40–$1.73/DL). The MMORPG genre slot is completely empty (zero Lineage, Black Desert, MU Awaken in any top 1,000). Korean IP works (Genshin Impact in all 6 markets, Solo Leveling in 5/6 markets).

Key risk: MMORPG complexity has not broken through despite Strategy 4X players being primed. Localization (Arabic-first, RTL UI) and a simplified onboarding are the primary barriers.

Recommended launch order: Saudi Arabia → UAE + Kuwait + Qatar (Gulf states) → Turkey (separate strategy)
